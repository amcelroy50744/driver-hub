Standard Operating Procedure

Dashboard Resources Pay Scale Delivery Guide
1

Receive Load Info

Verify the following from Dispatch:

  • Trailer & Store Number
  • Release & Deliver By Times
  • Round Trip Miles
2

Inbound Guard Shack

Provide Trailer # and Store # to receive your spot location and paperwork.

3

Pre-Trip & Macros

1. Complete Pre-Trip on the SmartSheet site.

Open SmartSheet Inspection

2. Complete Macros 1, 2, and 3.

Stop at Guard Shack for seal check/paperwork verification before exiting.
4

Store Arrival

Upon arrival at the store, complete Macro 4.

⚠️

Kingpin Safety Check

Before moving the dock trailer, verify it has a BLUE kingpin lock.

NO LOCK or RED LOCK?
DO NOT MOVE. Call Dispatch immediately for a safety verification.
5

The Double Drop & Hook

1. Move the dock trailer to the side.

2. Place loaded trailer in the EXACT same spot.

Trailer must be flush with the dock and perfectly straight.
6

Lockbox & Paperwork

  • Sign paperwork & place in lockbox.
  • Retrieve RED kingpin lock.
  • Place RED lock on loaded trailer.
  • Place BLUE lock inside the lockbox.
Complete Pinlock Submission
7

Final Departure

  • Complete Macro 14
  • Complete Post-Trip on SmartSheet
  • Complete Macro 5
Final SmartSheet Post-Trip
